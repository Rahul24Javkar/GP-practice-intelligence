We provide Antenatal Shared Care Services and a Children's Developmental and Immunisation Clinic. Total Healthcare for Everyone At gpbondi we want you to feel that your health needs are being met at the highest standards of care and adequate time is spent with a doctor in the delivery of your health care. We are not a medical centre. We are a specialist primary care centre focusing on whole-person health care. Our holistic care involves looking at all things that influence our health and wellbeing, including the physical, emotional, social, spiritual, nutritional and environmental determinants of health.
